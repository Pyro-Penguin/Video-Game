# World and Story Concepts

**Status:** Brainstorm consolidation; no canon plot or world map finalized.

## Central Theme
A powerful, gluttonous figure rises in a coup involving AI. Two mythical beings associated with Light and Shadow frame the central question: is a being inherently evil because of its origin or initial behavior, or does moral responsibility belong to the person directing and using it? Is redemption possible?

## Seven-Village Structure
An early concept proposes seven starting villages, potentially associated with seven primary typings. Each village may have its own starter creature and distinct culture. Another concept describes three clans per village; the complete mapping is not defined.

## Player Origin and Identity
The player's origin village may remain meaningful through unique access, identity, or abilities, while later quests and learned moves permit broader team-building flexibility. Formal progression design is open.

## Story and World Hooks
- A strange old man / dimensional "magic man" introduces a portal-based system and may be the first companion.
- A "Portal Gauntlet" is proposed as the primary means of capturing monsters.
- The old man may enable appearance/class respecialization through dimensional transformation.
- The protagonist's mother may be a gym/arena leader. A plot incident could lead to her death and the protagonist inheriting the role, with periodic challenger encounters.
- The player may begin in any gym town, with gyms and creatures scaling to progression.
- NPCs may participate in tournaments as contenders or spectators, affecting reputation.
- A nemesis-like system could support recurring rivals and challengers.

All are concepts. Compatibility, tone, and narrative order remain open.
