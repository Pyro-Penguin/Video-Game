# Types and Villages

**Status:** Exploratory type inventory. No final type chart or seven-type subset approved.

## Type Names Mentioned
- Fire
- Plant
- Water
- Dragon
- Metal
- Celestial
- Earth
- Electricity
- Air
- Light
- Shadow
- Prismatic

The first nine appear in a rough three-row grouping; Light, Shadow, and Prismatic appear as an additional trio. It is unclear whether these are all battle types, village identities, special affinities, or overlapping categories.

## Seven-Village Proposal
Seven initial villages, each with its own starter, is a recurring structure idea. Exact village/type pairings are unresolved.

## Special Mythical Pair
Light and Shadow are proposed as paired mythical creatures with narrative significance. This does not determine whether Light and Shadow are ordinary player-accessible types.

## Balance Requirements
- Define the complete type roster.
- Define attack and defense interactions.
- Decide whether the chart is symmetric or asymmetric.
- Define immunity separately from resistance.
- Test matchup cycles and cross-triangle interactions.
- Ensure village identity remains meaningful without forcing unbalanced team composition.

Do not build a type chart from the rough list without an explicit design pass.
