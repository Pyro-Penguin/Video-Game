# Exploration, Capture, and Utility Concepts

**Status:** Candidate gameplay features; no implementation or progression rules approved.

## Portal Gauntlet and Capture
A strange old man from another dimension may give the player a **Portal Gauntlet**, proposed as the primary means of capturing monsters. The same character may serve as the player's first companion and provide a story explanation for appearance/class respecialization.

The capture rules, portal fiction, failure conditions, and relationship to monster habitats remain open.

## Ability-Driven Environmental Puzzles
Combat abilities should ideally be usable outside combat:
- **Move:** telekinetically push pillars or objects across gaps.
- **Freeze:** freeze puddles or water into scalable ice structures.
- **Whirlwind:** clear heavy overgrowth or uncover hidden passages.

This is intended to connect party composition and ability acquisition to dungeon traversal. The exact abilities and environmental physics are not finalized.

## Shrinking and Growing
A mystical portal may allow the player to shrink to insect scale. Ordinary spaces—cracks, puddles, furniture, and rooms—could become large traversal zones or dungeons.

## Lock-and-Key Progression
Tools such as a hookshot-like traversal device or bombs may function both as combat tools and as keys to new areas. This can create a loop of curiosity, acquisition, and backtracking.

## Creature Traversal
Some monsters may be rideable or act as steeds, subject to size requirements. Define whether rideability is a species property, individual trait, learned ability, or equipment interaction.

## Encounter and Party Convenience
- A Dimengeon-like encounter management system is a candidate way to reduce interruptions during exploration.
- One-button Auto-Heal is a candidate quality-of-life feature to reduce repetitive menu navigation.
- The main monster may follow the player; an additional companion may optionally travel with the player or their chosen monster.

## Open Questions
- Is monster capture primarily portal-based, habitat-based, or a combination?
- Can every monster be captured through the gauntlet?
- How do monsters' abilities unlock new traversal paths?
- Are puzzles solvable with the current party, or do they require specific species?
- How does shrinking affect combat, traversal, and camera presentation?
- How do rideable size classes work?
- Does Auto-Heal consume items/resources or only restore at safe locations?
