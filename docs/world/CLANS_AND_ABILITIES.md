# Clans and Abilities

**Status:** Partial village/clan concepts. Incomplete and unbalanced; not approved mechanics.

## Water Village — Divided Village Concept
- **Storm Clan — Storm:** Summon Rain for 7 turns.
- **Clan of Mist — Shroud:** Summon Mist for 7 turns.
- **Ice Clan — Flurry:** Summon Snow for 7 turns.

Weather duration and whether these are weather, terrain, or another battlefield state need definition.

## Village of the Sun
- **Flamecloak Clan (Defensive):** Instead of an activated ability, members receive a Flame Cloak item. The clan is described as the emperor's direct guard since the village's foundation. Their cloaks burn creatures that make physical contact.
- **Order of the Sacred Flame — Illuminate:** Summon Sun for 7 turns.
- **Clan of the Primal Spark — Eruption:** STAB is 2x instead of 1.5x.

The Eruption entry is incomplete in the source notes; the STAB comparison is a concept, not a final global rule.

## Burn Status — Draft Concept
- A burned creature takes damage at end of every turn unless cured.
- Burned creatures take 50% more Fire damage.
- Burn damage itself does not count as Fire damage.
- A creature begins at "1st degree burn".
- Each time a burned creature is hit by Fire damage, its burn increases one degree, up to degree 4.
- Each degree increases end-of-turn damage.

Missing: exact damage per degree, stacking/refresh behavior, cure methods, duration, immunity, and modifier order.
