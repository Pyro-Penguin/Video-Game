# Player Character and Class Concepts

**Status:** Provisional design direction; no class or stat formula finalized.

## Player as a Combatant
The player character is intended to participate directly in battles alongside monster companions. One draft proposes that the player's base-stat total should be comparable to the strongest Mega-form monsters in Pokémon Champions. Treat this as a balance benchmark for investigation, not a confirmed numerical target.

## Candidate Combat Archetypes
The supplied class-role list includes:
- Physical attacker.
- Special attacker.
- Physical tank.
- Special tank.

This is a role taxonomy, not yet a finalized class list. More specialized classes may exist later, but should not be created before the core stat and equipment model is defined.

## Class Identity
A possible direction is for classes to work more like monster natures than deep RPG skill trees: a class creates a meaningful stat tradeoff and influences access to abilities or equipment. Earlier project notes also propose equipment compatibility, such as weapon requirements and restrictions on armor or oversized weapons. Those details remain provisional and need to be consolidated into a formal ruleset.

## Build Systems Mentioned
- Base stats.
- Nature-like stat tradeoffs.
- IV-like individual variation.
- Equipment and weapons.
- Class-specific abilities or move access.
- Possible respecialization through the dimensional old man / Portal Gauntlet story device.

## Design Questions
1. Is the player a single unit on the field, a commander outside the active formation, or both depending on mode?
2. Does the player's stat budget use the same formula as monsters?
3. Is the Mega-stat benchmark a total-stat target, a role-specific comparison, or merely a power ceiling?
4. Are classes selected at character creation, freely changed, or changed through a story system?
5. Which stats can a class modify, and are tradeoffs mandatory?
6. Are class restrictions tied to weapons, armor, abilities, or all three?
7. How do story progression and competitive normalization interact?

## Guardrail
Do not finalize player stats from the Light/Shadow draft. First define a common stat model and a clear relationship between player and monster combat roles.
