# Open Questions

## Blocking Battle Implementation
1. Does the commander count as one of the three selected units, or are three monsters selected in addition to the commander?
2. Is the five-monster preview pool separate from the commander?
3. What exactly ends a match: commander knockout, all monsters defeated, or another condition?
4. Can the commander take damage directly, and what protection/interception rules apply?
5. What triggers automatic reserve entry, and when does it occur?
6. What actions are available to the commander and each active monster each turn?
7. How are simultaneous actions ordered when speed, priority, or dependencies conflict?
8. What happens to an action whose target is knocked out earlier in the resolution sequence?
9. What are the normalized stat baselines and allowed customization?
10. What are the damage equation, rounding rules, and random variance policy?
11. What are the elemental types and complete effectiveness multipliers?
12. What are the exact draw and timeout conditions?

## Blocking Project Integration
1. What Unity editor version is installed and intended for this project?
2. Where is the actual Unity project folder, and does it already contain a valid `Assets/`, `Packages/`, and `ProjectSettings/` structure?
3. Which existing project archive is the canonical starting point, if any?
4. Are there existing scripts or assets that should be preserved and integrated rather than rewritten?

## Deferred Until Battle Core Is Stable
- Final visual direction and production asset pipeline.
- Full campaign/world structure and village-specific progression.
- Online multiplayer and server authority.
- Save format and long-term progression persistence.
- Localization, analytics, monetization, and live operations.
