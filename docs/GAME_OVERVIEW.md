# Game Overview

**Status:** Working concept; not a finalized production specification.

## High-Level Identity
An original creature-collecting RPG combining party-based JRPG adventure, tactical turn-based combat, monster collection, and competitive PvP. The project draws on familiar genre conventions but intends to develop its own world, creatures, mechanics, terminology, and story.

## Core Player Fantasy
Explore a magical world, build relationships with a team of creatures, develop a distinctive player character, solve environmental puzzles using abilities that also matter in combat, and test the resulting team in tactical battles.

## Conceptual Pillars
1. Creature partnership and individual monster identity.
2. A player character who is a meaningful combatant.
3. Exploration puzzles using the same abilities found in combat.
4. Tactical expression through team composition, elements, equipment, and actions.
5. A living world with villages, clans, rivals, tournaments, and NPC reactions.
6. An original story about responsibility, power, creation, and redemption.

## Battle Concept — Conflicting Drafts
Notes include a player plus three selected monsters from five; a 3v3 format with five monsters brought to preview and three selected; a commander plus two active monsters and one reserve per side; and an earlier shorthand 2v2 description. These are not reconciled. See [Battle Overview](battle/BATTLE_OVERVIEW.md).

## Story Premise
A powerful, gluttonous figure rises through a coup involving AI. Two mythical creatures associated with Light and Shadow are central to a story asking whether a created being is inherently evil because of its origin or initial behavior, or whether responsibility belongs to the person wielding it—and whether redemption is possible.

This is a thematic premise, not a finalized plot. Antagonist identity, coup mechanics, mythical creatures' agency, and protagonist role remain open.

## World and Progression Hooks
Seven starting villages with unique starters; possible three-clan structure per village; meaningful origin identity with later flexibility through quests and learned moves; a strange dimensional old man who grants a Portal Gauntlet and may enable respecialization; gym/arena leadership, recurring challengers, tournaments, and a nemesis-like rival system.

All are concepts, not approved features.

## Development Strategy
There is no Unity implementation yet. Consolidate concepts, run isolated mechanics experiments, resolve battle-format contradictions, specify a testable prototype, and only then establish the engine project.
