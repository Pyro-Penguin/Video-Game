# Art and Audio Inspiration

**Status:** Directional references only; no asset pipeline or style guide finalized.

## Visual References
- **Gris:** expressive color, atmosphere, emotional visual storytelling.
- **Alto's Odyssey:** serene landscapes, environmental flow, and mood.
- **Therese Nielsen:** illustrative fantasy sensibility.
- **Seb McKinnon:** evocative, painterly fantasy imagery.

These are references for qualities and mood, not instructions to reproduce any artist's or game's protected style. The project needs its own visual identity.

## Audio
Adaptive music is a candidate feature:
- Peaceful exploration music transitions into more energetic battle music.
- Transition should feel seamless and react to gameplay state.
- Music may layer or change intensity rather than abruptly switching tracks.
