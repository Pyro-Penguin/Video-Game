# Gameplay References

**Status:** Reference inventory; no feature is committed solely by appearing here.

## Monument Valley — Environmental Puzzles
Interest: compact, readable spaces where perspective and spatial relationships create puzzle solutions.

## JRPG Psynergy-Style Ability Integration
The same abilities used in battle can manipulate the world:
- Move: push objects or pillars.
- Freeze: turn water into climbable ice structures.
- Whirlwind: clear heavy growth or reveal passages.

Underlying principle: party abilities should have consistent rules across combat and exploration.

## Zelda-Style Lock and Key
Tools acquired through progression serve both as combat options and keys to previously inaccessible spaces, creating curiosity, acquisition, and backtracking.

## Shrinking and Growing
A portal or magical ability could shrink the party to insect scale. Ordinary cracks, puddles, furniture, and household spaces become traversal environments or dungeons.

## Dimengeon Machine
Interest in contained encounter storage/management that reduces exploration interruption. Exact implementation and fit with the fiction are open.

## Nemesis-Style Recurring Rivals
Recurring NPCs develop history with the player and return in different roles. Possible applications include tournament rivals and challengers to inherited village leadership. Feasibility and scope are unresolved.

## Streamlined Party Management
A one-button Auto-Heal could reduce repetitive menu navigation. Resource rules and separation between story and competitive modes need definition.

## Rideable Creatures
Some creatures may serve as mounts or steeds, subject to size and traversal requirements.

## Tournament World Simulation
NPCs may appear as tournament contenders or spectators. Their participation and the player's results could affect local reputation.
