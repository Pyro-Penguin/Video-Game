# Creature Design Concepts

**Status:** Brainstorm inventory; no roster schema or species catalogue finalized.

## Creature Roles and Behaviors
- Ghost-like creatures may mimic ordinary objects, creating discovery, surprise, and environmental storytelling.
- Some creatures may be rideable or function as steeds, subject to size requirements.
- The player's main monster may visibly follow during exploration.
- A second companion may optionally accompany the player or their chosen monster.

## Progression and Build Concepts
Potential mechanics:
- Base stats.
- Natures or nature-like tradeoffs.
- IV-like individual variation.
- Weather and terrain effects.
- Level normalization for competitive battle.
- Mega-like transformations or enhanced forms, referenced in Light/Shadow stat notes.

These are candidate systems, not approved mechanics. Competitive normalization must prevent collection/progression from creating unfair PvP advantages.

## Light and Shadow Mythical Stat Drafts
Supplied notes contain base-stat-like values, Mega values, and final Level 100 values. They are preserved as unverified source material in [the stat appendix](LIGHT_SHADOW_STAT_DRAFT.md). Internal inconsistencies mean none should be treated as canonical.

## Future Data Needs
A creature definition may eventually need stable identity, typing, base stats, abilities, learnset, form/transform data, visual references, and traversal eligibility. Define the schema after battle rules and progression.
